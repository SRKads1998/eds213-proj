# Hip-Hop Songs and Samples: Vizualizing Sound

### Purpose
This repo is contains the data, database, and quarto doc needed to analyze the contained hip-hop songs. By querying the partnered tables, we are able to understand the total run-time of the hip-hop songs when you add the samples utilized for their production, as well as obtain other interesting statistics pertaining to them. 

We used a variety of different songs and albums across different eras of hip-hop history, which gave us a breadth of data to analyse. While older albums, such as Madvilliany and Modal Soul, were sample heavy, much of the production in the newer albums had fewer samples, with some entirely devoid of them with entirely new music.

All data is accessible and freely downloadable from this repository. The three csvs utilized as the dataset are all original creations by myself. Song information was collected from open sources, and sample lists were found by searching individual song names on the WhoSampled.com website.
